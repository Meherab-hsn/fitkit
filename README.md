# FitKit

A fitness companion Android app built with **Java** and **Firebase** — plan workouts, track food and water, check your body metrics, and get AI-powered help.

## Features

- **Login & Signup** — secure access with Firebase Authentication
- **Dashboard** — your fitness overview in one place
- **Exercise** — workout section to guide your training
- **Nutrition & Recipes** — food tracking and recipe ideas
- **Water** — daily water-intake tracking
- **Body Fat & Metabolic** — body-fat percentage and metabolic-rate calculators
- **AI Assistant** — smart fitness responses powered by Gemini

## Tech Stack

- Java (Android)
- Firebase
- Gemini AI
